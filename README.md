# 🔧 Tech Fix Connect

> An affordable, convenient, and student-focused device repair platform.

## 📖 About the Project

Tech Fix Connect is a web-based platform designed to help university students quickly and affordably repair their electronic devices. Students rely heavily on laptops, smartphones, and tablets for their academic work, and a broken device can negatively affect their studies.

Our platform allows students to book repair services, track the progress of their repairs, explore available services, and contact the repair team through a simple and user-friendly website.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

University students often struggle to find affordable and reliable repair services when their devices break. Existing repair options are usually expensive, time-consuming, or located off campus, causing students to miss deadlines and disrupt their academic progress.

Tech Fix Connect addresses this problem by providing a convenient repair service specifically designed for students.
